The 808 stops for one bar in the middle of each verse (bar 8, at 0:32 and 1:13). That's a good spot for a punchline.

## How drill flows sit on this beat

- **The snare lands on "3".** Count "1, 2, **3**, 4". The clap hits on 3 every bar. Landing your rhymes on that snare makes bars hit hard.
- **Drill feels slow even though it's 142 BPM**, because the snare only hits once per bar. Rap at a relaxed pace; don't rush.
- **Love drill = vulnerable lyrics over a hard beat.** Talk about someone you miss, trust issues, or wanting better for the two of you. The contrast is what makes it work.
- **Hook rule:** short, repeatable, one main idea. If you can't sing it after hearing it twice, simplify it.
- **Verse rule:** rhyme the last word of every 2 lines (or every line), and save your strongest line for bar 16, right before the hook.
